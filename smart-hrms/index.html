FAQ

❔ Frequently asked questions

Is Smart HRMS free?

Smart HRMS is currently in early access at no cost. Pricing will be announced before general availability — early access users will get preferred rates.

Does face recognition work without internet?

Yes — face recognition runs on-device using edge AI. Attendance syncs when connectivity is restored.

Is it compliant with Indian labour laws?

Smart HRMS supports PF, ESI, professional tax and state-specific regulations out of the box. We keep up with policy changes so you don't have to.

How many employees can I onboard?

Smart HRMS scales from 5 to 5,000+ employees. It's designed for MSMEs but handles growth gracefully.

Can I migrate from my existing HR software?

Yes — we provide CSV import for employee data, attendance history and leave balances. Our team helps with migration during early access.
