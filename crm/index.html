FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Brilliants CRM free?

Brilliants CRM is live and free to start at crm.brilliants.in — contacts, pipeline, tickets and basic campaigns included. Paid features, when introduced, will be clearly listed before you use them.

Does it need installation?

No. Brilliants CRM runs entirely in the browser on desktop, tablet and mobile — just open crm.brilliants.in and sign in.

How do the automatic follow-ups work?

You define a rule — for example "if a lead stays New for 3 days, send a friendly reminder." The engine checks running jobs regularly and sends from your connected mailbox, logging each send in the outbox.

Can I import my existing contacts?

Yes — bulk import from CSV is supported, and duplicate entries are merged automatically.

Who owns the data?

Your contacts and conversations belong to your organisation. Access is protected by login and row-level security, and export is always available.
