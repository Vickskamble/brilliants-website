1 Parties
This Partner Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Brilliants Technology ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"), having its registered office at Hinganghat, Maharashtra 442301, India, and the partner entity or individual ("Partner," "you," or "your") identified in the partner profile submitted through the Brilliants Partner Program registration process.
2 Purpose
The purpose of this Agreement is to establish the terms and conditions under which the Partner may participate in the Brilliants Partner Program to refer, sell, and/or implement Brilliants Technology solutions including but not limited to Power EmS, IronBook, Business Software, AI & Automation, and IoT Solutions to prospective customers.
3 Partner Role
The Partner's role and level of participation under this Agreement shall be determined by the capability selected during the registration process: (a) Refer + Sell (Sales Partner) or (b) Refer + Sell + Implement (Solution Partner). The Partner's capability level determines the revenue share structure, responsibilities, and obligations as outlined in this Agreement.
4 Refer + Sell Responsibilities
Partners operating at the Refer + Sell (Sales Partner) level agree to: (a) Identify and qualify prospective customers who may benefit from Brilliants Technology solutions; (b) Introduce Brilliants solutions to qualified prospects; (c) Participate in product demonstrations and presentations as needed; (d) Support the sales process through to customer closure; (e) Coordinate with Brilliants team members throughout the sales cycle; (f) Provide accurate and timely information about prospective customers and market conditions.
5 Refer + Sell + Implement Responsibilities
Partners operating at the Refer + Sell + Implement (Solution Partner) level agree to all responsibilities outlined for Sales Partners, plus: (a) Handle customer implementation and deployment of Brilliants solutions; (b) Provide agreed-upon technical support to customers during and after implementation; (c) Coordinate with Brilliants technical team for complex deployments; (d) Ensure customer satisfaction throughout the implementation process; (e) Maintain technical competency through Brilliants-provided training and resources; (f) Comply with Brilliants implementation standards and best practices.
6 Revenue Share
Revenue share is determined by the Partner's capability level: (a) Sales Partner (Refer + Sell): 10% revenue share on qualifying customer subscriptions; (b) Solution Partner (Refer + Sell + Implement): Up to 20% revenue share on qualifying customer subscriptions. The exact revenue share percentage within the "up to 20%" range for Solution Partners shall be determined based on factors including implementation complexity, customer lifecycle value, and individual partner performance, as mutually agreed upon between Brilliants and the Partner. Revenue share applies only to qualifying subscriptions as defined by Brilliants from time to time.
7 Recurring Revenue
When a customer introduced by the Partner continues with an eligible Brilliants subscription and renews, the Partner may continue to earn revenue share on eligible renewals, subject to: (a) The Partner maintaining active partner status under this Agreement; (b) The customer subscription remaining in good standing; (c) Compliance with all applicable partner terms and conditions; (d) Revenue share on renewals is contingent upon the Partner's continued participation in the Partner Program and adherence to all applicable terms.
8 Lead Registration
Partners must register prospective customer leads through the Brilliants Partner Dashboard using the designated lead registration form. All required fields must be completed accurately. Partners are responsible for ensuring that lead information is current, accurate, and obtained with appropriate consent from the prospective customer. Brilliants reserves the right to verify lead information and may reject leads that do not meet qualification criteria or are found to contain inaccurate information.
9 Lead Protection
Once a lead is registered by a Partner in the system, that lead is protected for the registering Partner for a period as determined by Brilliants. Lead protection means that other Partners or Brilliants direct sales will not override a legitimately registered lead during the protection period, provided the Partner is actively working the opportunity in good faith. Lead protection is forfeited if: (a) The Partner fails to actively progress the opportunity; (b) The lead information is found to be inaccurate or misleading; (c) The protection period expires without meaningful sales activity; (d) The lead was already registered by another Partner or exists in Brilliants' direct sales pipeline.
10 Customer Relationship
The Partner acknowledges that the end customer relationship is primarily between Brilliants and the customer. While the Partner may facilitate introductions and support the sales and implementation process, Brilliants retains ultimate responsibility for customer relationship management, product delivery, billing, and support. Partners shall not make representations, warranties, or commitments on behalf of Brilliants without prior written authorization.
11 Payment / Commission
Revenue share payments ("Commissions") will be calculated and paid on a monthly or quarterly basis as determined by Brilliants. Commissions are based on qualifying customer subscription revenue actually received by Brilliants. Payments are made via bank transfer or other agreed-upon method. Partners are responsible for providing accurate payment details and maintaining current banking information. Brilliants will provide commission statements detailing the calculation basis. Partners are responsible for all applicable taxes on commission payments.
12 Implementation Responsibilities
Solution Partners who handle customer implementation agree to: (a) Complete required Brilliants training and certification before undertaking implementations; (b) Follow Brilliants implementation methodologies and best practices; (c) Provide implementation timelines and project plans to customers and Brilliants; (d) Escalate technical issues to Brilliants support as appropriate; (e) Ensure customer systems meet minimum requirements for Brilliants solutions; (f) Maintain appropriate technical skills and resources to deliver quality implementations; (g) Comply with all Brilliants quality standards and customer satisfaction requirements.
13 Support
Partners are expected to provide first-level support to their customers for issues related to implementations they have delivered. Brilliants will provide partner-level technical support, documentation, training resources, and escalation paths for issues that require Brilliants engineering involvement. Support levels and response times will be as defined in the Partner Support documentation provided by Brilliants.
14 Confidentiality
The Partner agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all non-public information disclosed by Brilliants, including but not limited to product roadmaps, pricing strategies, customer data, business plans, technical specifications, and proprietary processes. This confidentiality obligation survives the termination of this Agreement for a period of two (2) years. The Partner shall not disclose confidential information to any third party without prior written consent from Brilliants.
15 Data Protection
The Partner shall comply with all applicable data protection laws and regulations, including but not limited to India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA) and any other applicable data protection legislation. The Partner shall: (a) Handle customer data in accordance with applicable data protection requirements; (b) Obtain necessary consents before collecting or processing personal data; (c) Implement appropriate security measures to protect personal data; (d) Report any data breaches to Brilliants promptly; (e) Cooperate with Brilliants on data subject requests and data protection inquiries.
16 Intellectual Property
All intellectual property rights in Brilliants products, technology, trademarks, trade names, logos, and marketing materials remain the exclusive property of Brilliants. The Partner is granted a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use Brilliants brand materials solely for the purpose of marketing and selling Brilliants solutions under this Agreement. The Partner shall not alter, modify, or create derivative works of Brilliants intellectual property without prior written consent.
17 Brand Usage
The Partner may use Brilliants brand name, logos, and trademarks solely in connection with marketing and promoting Brilliants solutions under this Agreement. All brand usage must comply with Brilliants brand guidelines as provided from time to time. The Partner shall not use Brilliants brand in any manner that could create confusion, imply unauthorized endorsement, or damage Brilliants' reputation. Upon termination of this Agreement, the Partner must immediately cease all use of Brilliants brand materials.
18 Marketing Rules
All marketing activities conducted by the Partner on behalf of or in connection with Brilliants solutions must be pre-approved by Brilliants. The Partner shall not make any false, misleading, or exaggerated claims about Brilliants products or services. Digital advertising, email campaigns, and social media posts referencing Brilliants must comply with applicable advertising standards and be submitted for review before publication. The Partner is responsible for ensuring compliance with all applicable marketing and advertising regulations.
19 Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party. Brilliants may terminate this Agreement immediately if the Partner: (a) Breaches any material term of this Agreement and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days of written notice; (b) Engages in fraudulent, deceptive, or unethical business practices; (c) Becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy; (d) Violates applicable laws or regulations. Upon termination, the Partner must cease all marketing and sales activities related to Brilliants solutions and return all confidential materials and brand assets.
20 Dispute / Governing Law
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall first be attempted to be resolved through good-faith negotiation between the parties. If negotiation fails, the dispute shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 of India, with the seat of arbitration being Maharashtra, India. The language of arbitration shall be English.
21 Agreement Duration
This Agreement shall commence on the date of signing and shall remain in effect until terminated by either party in accordance with Section 19 (Termination). The Agreement may be renewed automatically for successive periods unless either party provides notice of non-renewal at least thirty (30) days before the end of the current term.
22 Amendments
Brilliants reserves the right to amend this Agreement from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to Partners via email or through the Partner Dashboard with at least thirty (30) days' advance notice. The Partner's continued participation in the Partner Program after the effective date of any amendments constitutes acceptance of the amended terms. If the Partner does not agree to the amended terms, the Partner may terminate this Agreement before the amendments take effect.
23 Applicable Terms
This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties with respect to the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written. If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect. The failure of either party to enforce any right or provision of this Agreement shall not constitute a waiver of such right or provision. This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, and all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
