All Features

🚀 Everything your energy needs

Power EmS turns smart meter data into clear insights — track usage, verify bills, catch anomalies and keep power quality in check.

📊 Live Energy Dashboard

Real-time view of energy consumption across meters. Track units, demand and costs at a glance with an always-current dashboard.

🔄 Smart Meter Insights

Deep insights from smart meter data — usage patterns, load profiles and trends that help you understand exactly where energy goes.

✓ Bill Accuracy Checks

Verify utility bills against your own meter readings. Detect overbilling, billing errors and discrepancies before you pay.

🛡️ Power Quality Monitoring

Monitor voltage, current and power quality parameters. Spot sags, swells and harmonics that damage equipment and inflate bills.

🔔 Anomaly Detection

Automatic detection of unusual consumption, tampering and leakages. Get alerted the moment something looks wrong.

📣 System Alerts

Timely notifications for abnormal readings, meter communication failures and threshold breaches — so nothing slips through.

📝 Meter Management

Register, organise and manage multiple meters from one place. Map meters to locations, consumers and tariff categories.

✎️ Manual & Smart Entry

Add readings manually or pull them from smart meters automatically. Flexible entry keeps data flowing even offline.

📈 Analysis & Reports

Compare consumption across months, meters and consumers. Export reports for audits, submissions and decision-making.

⚡ Energy Analytics

Consumption analysis by period, tariff and category. Spot seasonal trends and optimise when and how you use energy.

🌎 Local-Ready

Built for Indian consumers and businesses — supports Indian tariffs, billing cycles and utility billing formats.

🔒 Secure by Design

Row-Level Security on all data, safe authentication and role-based access — your energy data stays private.

Tech Stack

Cross-platform UI framework. Same codebase for Android, Web, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux.

Open-source Firebase alternative. PostgreSQL, Auth, Realtime, Storage — all in one.

Modern, fast, type-safe language behind Flutter — reliable and performant.

📄 Reports Engine

Export-ready energy reports for audits, submissions and decision-making.

Row-Level Security and role-based access keep every meter's data private.
