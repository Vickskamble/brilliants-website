Common questions

Questions business owners ask us

Do I have to know technology to work with BRILLIANTS?

Not at all. Most of our customers run their businesses from Excel and WhatsApp. We start from your business process, not from a tech spec — you'll understand every step in plain language.

Do you always recommend software to a business?

No. If a process change or a small tool solves the problem without new software, we'll tell you. We earn trust by giving honest advice — not by selling licenses.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on the problem. A focused automation workflow can go live in weeks. Larger systems are built in phases, starting with the part of your business that will show value first.

What does it cost to get started?

IronBook starts free with a plan for up to 25 members. For custom automation and software work we scope the project after understanding your process — so you only pay for what solves your problem. See our pricing page.

Is my business data safe?

Yes. Our systems are cloud-hosted with encrypted connections and role-based access — every business's data stays isolated and its workforce sees only what you authorize. You always own your data and can export it.

Is BRILLIANTS a product company or a services company?

Both. We build and own our products — IronBook, Smart HRMS, Smart Billing and the AI engine. And we build custom solutions with you when your problem is unique. Either way, the approach is the same: understand your business first.

What happens after the solution is implemented?

We don't leave. We stay available by WhatsApp and email for support, and we monitor and improve the solution along with running business changes. A long-term partnership, not a hand-off.
